Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026. This policy describes the current release. It must be reviewed if analytics, ads, accounts, or new integrations are enabled.

Information used by the game

Night Rush 3D currently runs in your browser without account registration. The game stores your personal-best score in your browser's localStorage on your own device. It uses browser audio features only when you turn sound on. The current game does not upload your driving session or score to a dedicated game server.

Hosting and technical data

The website's hosting provider may process ordinary access information, such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs, as part of providing and securing the site. Such processing is governed by the provider's own policies.

Advertising and cookies

The current release does not include AdSense advertising tags. If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is enabled in the future, this policy and any applicable consent controls must be updated before activation. Such providers may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses, or similar technologies to show, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted. For information on Google's use of information, see Google's partner-site information and Google Ad Settings.

Deleting your local record

You can clear the site's local storage using browser settings to remove the saved personal-best score. This also resets the record on that browser.
