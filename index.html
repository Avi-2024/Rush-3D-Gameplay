QUESTIONS

Before you race

Is Night Rush 3D really free?

Yes. The game is free to play in your browser without an account or download.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, on mobile browsers that support WebGL. Performance depends on your device and browser.

Is there multiplayer?

Not currently. This release is a single-player endless highway racing game.

How are my records saved?

Your personal best is stored locally in your browser. Clearing site storage or using another browser may reset it.
